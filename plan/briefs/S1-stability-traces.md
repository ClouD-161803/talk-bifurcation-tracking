# S1: the traces, the region names, and the asinh scale of the stability demo

Read `plan/briefs/DECK-COPY.md` first, and work with the tag `stability`. The stability demo of slides 14 to 17 is a plot element of the template, built by `demos/stability/build.py` from `data/stability/`. Its JSON is inline in the deck in the block `<script type="application/json" id="stability">`, the element is `src/plot.ts` of the template, `/Users/claudiovestini/git/slides`, and its marks are documented under "Plots" in the README of the template. Claudio asked three things.

1. The traces of the colored dots. In the panel `circle`, the two pairs of multipliers are the marks `pair-1` and `pair-2` of kind `points`, which leave no trace, while the multipliers of the template's demo (slide 18 of the demo deck, built by `demo/multipliers.mjs`) are traces. Draw a trace for each of the four multipliers, i.e. four marks of kind `trace` in the tone of their pair, from the same data as the pairs (`tab_data` in `build.py` writes `pair-1` as `[x1, y1, x2, y2]` per frame, and a trace takes one `[x, y]` per frame). Show them at the same fragments as the pairs, i.e. add them to every `data-plot-show` that lists `pair-1 pair-2` on slides 14 to 17. A trace is drawn as the whole path in a faint line, the path up to the frame in full, and a dot at its end. Decide whether the faint whole path helps or distracts on the circle, where the four paths cross, and if it distracts, add an option `ghost: false` to the trace mark in `src/plot.ts`, documented in the README's table of marks and covered by a test in `test/`, in a worktree of the template on the branch `talk/plot-ghost`. Check that no trace cuts a chord across the circle where a pair leaves or reaches it, or where the order of the two multipliers of a pair swaps. If one does, order the multipliers in `build.py` so that the order is continuous, as `pair()` of `demo/multipliers.mjs` does, and say so. Mention the traces in the notes of slide 14 where the dots are introduced.
2. The names of the regions. The symbols of Frauenfelder and Moreno in `SYMBOL` of `build.py` are set in `\mathrm`, and Claudio wants them calligraphic. Write them with `\mathcal`, i.e. `\mathcal{E}^{2}`, `\mathcal{N}`, `\mathcal{H}^{-+}`, `\mathcal{H}^{++}`, `\mathcal{H}^{--}`, `\mathcal{EH}^{+}`, and `\mathcal{EH}^{-}`, and check the readout of the region on slides 15 to 17 in both modes.
3. The asinh scale. Claudio is confused by it, and he asks that it be explained if it makes sense and removed otherwise. It makes sense. On the four tabs 11P, 11A, 8P, and 8A, the coefficients run to $10^4$ and $10^5$ (on 11A, $a_1$ runs from −5621 to 3307 and $a_2$ from −50930 to 9836), while the stable region and the lines $k = \pm 2$ are within a few units of the origin, hence a linear axis would show nothing of the path near the origin, and a logarithm cannot take a negative value. Keep the scale, with the tabs 7P and 7A linear as they are, and explain it on the slide and in the notes. At the fragment of the slide that shows the diagram (`data-plot-show="diagram path point"`), add a small line in the thesis style, e.g. "The axes of families 8 and 11 are on an asinh scale, \(\operatorname{asinh} a = \ln\bigl(a + \sqrt{a^2 + 1}\bigr)\), which is \(a\) near zero and \(\pm\ln 2|a|\) far from it." In the matching step of the notes, replace "Both axes are on an asinh scale, since a₁ and a₂ span several decades along this family." by an explanation in words: the scale is linear within about one unit of zero and logarithmic beyond, i.e. a signed logarithm that passes through zero, the ticks are at ±2, ±10, ±100, and so on, and family 7 fits on a linear scale, hence its two tabs are linear. Check that the title of the diagram axis, which `build.py` writes with "on an asinh scale", agrees with the line.
