## The decisions, finding by finding

The findings are cited as C2 to C12 for the council report and as V1 and V2 for the verification report.

1. C2, C3, and C6, the orbit panels of scene 2. Replace the two orbit panels by one, which spans the lower row, and in which the members of both pens are drawn. Draw every earlier member in dim gray, and the newest member of each pen in the color of its pen, orange or blue, so that the two pens are told apart without a second panel. The cividis colormap and its colorbar go, hence the empty blue panel of shots 1 and 2 and the khaki members of shot 3 go with them. Give the close-up the larger share of the top row, about 60% of its width, since the payoff of slide 13 is in it, and keep the diagram at the left.
2. C3, C7, and V2, the fold. Remove the corrector path of a failed attempt of either pen, and keep its predictor, drawn faintly, and its red dot. Keep the corrector path of a successful attempt. With the one orbit panel of decision 1, at most two things then move at the fold, the two pens, and the newest member is updated in one panel. If something else still moves or glows in the shots of the fold, hold it or dim it, and say so in the report.
3. V1, the picture of the pseudo-arclength constraint. On family 11 the diagram plots $x_0$ against $e$, in which the two branches of the fold are less than $10^{-4}$ apart and the turn of the tangent is invisible, since the family is continued through the fold in the launch velocity $\dot{y}_0$ alone. Plot $\dot{y}_0$ against $e$ for family 11 instead, in which the fold is a smooth turn, and set the weights of the continuation norm on the two coordinates of the diagram only, i.e. zero on $x_0$ and on the period, so that the tangent and the constraint line drawn in the diagram are the tangent and the constraint of the method, and the drawn corrector path is normal to the drawn tangent. For the Lyapunov family, plot $\dot{y}_0$ against $C$ as well if its curve reads as well as the present one, and otherwise keep $x_0$ against $C$ with the weights on $x_0$ and $C$. Title the ordinate axis by what it plots. Refit the close-up to the fold in the new coordinates, verify that the blue pen passes the fold at the three step sizes and that the orange pen is stopped at it as before, and report the halvings of each pen at the fold. If the blue pen stalls under these weights, keep the present weights, do not draw the constraint line of the blue pen, and say so in the report, since the slide is then worded differently.
4. C4, the sixteen segments of shot 3. Stage iterate 0 of a run with $S > 1$, which spans three units of the clock: draw one segment first, i.e. its arc, its velocity sticks, and its defect, with the other segments dim, then draw the other arcs and defects together, and then move all the nodes at once. Hold the convergence panel until the end of iterate 0. Keep the later iterates as they are, in which all the segments move together.
5. C5, the offset of the first guess. Rename the offset from $\delta$ to $\varepsilon$ everywhere, i.e. in the tabs of scene 1, the storyboard, the README, `CHECKS.md`, and the text and the notes of slides 10 and 11, since $\delta$ is the Newton step on slide 10. On slide 11, say with the judge: "I make a deliberately worse first guess by scaling its positions and velocities by 5%, so ε = 0.05."
6. C8, the head of the convergence curve. Snap the head to the computed points, so that it does not slide between integer iterates, and update the caption when each point is added.
7. C9, the ghosts. Draw the ghosts below the current arcs and at a lower opacity, and keep the plasma color by $n$, which ties a ghost to its point of the convergence curve. Draw the current arcs and the comet on top.
8. C10, the reference solution. When the solution is toggled on, draw the reference above the arcs and the ghosts, as a wider dashed gray line.
9. C11, the tangent past the fold on slide 13. Replace the sentence "Past the fold, the sign of τₑ is flipped." by "I keep the tangent oriented forward. Its eccentricity component passes through zero and changes sign beyond the fold.", in the slide and in its notes.
10. C12, the weights on slide 13. Rewrite the where clause of the bordered system and the sentence of the notes for the weights of decision 3, in short sentences, with the launch velocity written $\dot{y}_0$ as in the storyboard.
