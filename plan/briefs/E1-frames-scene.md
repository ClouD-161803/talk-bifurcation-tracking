## The scene

- The mass ratio is μ = 0.5, Broucke's, so that the family 7 orbit of `examples/broucke/broucke-input.tyd` fits. At this μ the point 𝓛₁ is at the barycenter and 𝓛₂ and 𝓛₃ mirror each other, which the storyboard says.
- The family parameter is the eccentricity e, on a slider. Take the e values of the family 7 periapsis branch from `broucke-input.tyd` (read its layout in `examples/broucke/build.py`), and add e = 0 when it is absent, so that the scene opens in the circular problem. If this grid is coarser than about 20 values between 0 and the largest e of the branch, add values without an orbit, and let the orbit toggle show nothing there (rule V2 allows an empty layer). Report the grid you chose.
- Per e, one member of each set over one forcing period, θ from 0 to 2π, with the channels `theta`, `t` (from Kepler's equation, so that a clock on `t` shows the primaries speed up at periapsis), and `rho`, where ρ(θ) = (1 − e²)/(1 + e cos θ) is the separation of the primaries. The driver is the secondary, at rest in the rotating-pulsating frame.
- The three frames are rotating-pulsating (x, y), rotating (ξ, η), and barycentric inertial (X, Y), with the axes labeled so, as on the Broucke page.
- The primary and the secondary are bodies with trails, labeled m₁ and m₂. The five libration points are bodies labeled 𝓛₁ to 𝓛₅, at their CR3BP positions in the rotating-pulsating frame (solve the quintic for 𝓛₂ and 𝓛₃ in NumPy, and place 𝓛₄ and 𝓛₅ at (0, ±√3/2) for μ = 0.5), with trails, so that they stay fixed in the pulsating panel, breathe along their rays in the rotating panel, and trace ellipses in the inertial panel.
- The grid is a track set of straight lines in the rotating-pulsating frame, drawn with `context="driver"`, dim, so that it is rigid in the pulsating panel and breathes in the rotating panel and breathes and turns in the inertial panel. Choose the spacing and the extent so that the grid frames the primaries and the libration points with a margin and stays legible at the smallest panel size of the deck (a half-slide zone of about 1100 by 600 CSS pixels).
- The family 7 orbit is a toggle (a choice variable, default off), the periapsis branch at the selected e, drawn in every panel with a head and a trail, over its whole period until closure, with the number K of forcing periods in a readout.
- Three panels: pulsating and rotating side by side on top, inertial below, or another layout if the storyboard justifies it. The clock is a choice between `theta` and `t`, with the transport visible, since the presenter drags the slider and switches the clock live. Not kiosk.
- Readouts: e, θ, t, ρ(θ), and K. Colormaps keyed on the frame, in the style of the Broucke page, on a dark page that matches the deck.
- Keep `frames.html` under about 1.5 MB.
