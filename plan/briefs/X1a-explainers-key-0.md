## What changed

Two fixes were made to the TyCHO runtime, in `theme/vendor/tycho/tycho.js` of the talk and in `/Users/claudiovestini/git/tycho/code/src/pytycho/_static/tycho.js`, which are the same file. The viewer is now given a key pressed while one of its tabs, switches, or buttons has the focus, unless the control uses the key itself. Hence the presenter rewinds the loop with the key 0 right after a click on a tab, without a click on a panel first. The ends of a colorbar over integers are now written as integers, hence the colorbar of $n$ is labeled 0 and 10 rather than 0.00 and 10.0. The pages `correction.html` and `continuation.html` were built before these fixes, and the runtime embedded in them is the old one.
