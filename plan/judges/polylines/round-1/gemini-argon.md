## Findings

1. On talk slides 1, 2, 3, 9, 18, and 22 and template slides 1, 12, and 22 in dark and light modes, I found a major pop between the last trace frame and the final still. Comparing `captures/s3-dark-z009.png` with `captures/s3-dark-zstill.png` and `deck/s18-dark-f023.png` with `deck/s18-dark-still.png`, I measured 514 and 2,043 settled edge pixels, respectively, that are dimmed by over 30 RGB sum units in the still. In `deck/crops/s1-dark-f059-stacking.png` versus `deck/crops/s1-dark-still-stacking.png`, I counted 720 pixels altered by over 30 channel units, and in `deck/s22-dark-f023.png` versus `deck/s22-dark-still.png` 1,806 settled pixels are altered at 12.0 s. During `Orbits.frame` in `src/orbits.ts`, finished strands are cached on the detached canvas `this.under` in completion-time order, whereas in `Orbits.redraw` strands are painted directly onto `this.base` in array order. On slide 1, orange strand 180 is finished at 3.73 s, covered at 6.0 s, and brought back over the violet manifold hairlines at 30.0 s. To fix both pops, remove `this.under` and stroke every started strand directly onto `this.base` in array order on each animation frame.

2. On talk slides 3, 9, and 18 in dark and light modes, I found a major defect in branch ordering and seed-orbit weight. In `deck/crops/s18-dark-still-loops.png` and `captures/s3-dark-zstill.png`, the innermost orbit at zero eccentricity $e = 0$ is brighter than adjacent curves, with 5.82:1 contrast at pixel (1164, 409) against 3.41:1 on the $e = 0.040$ orbit. In `backdrops/export_broucke_families.py`, the circular three-body seed orbit is kept in both `periapsis` and `apoapsis`, so the same curve is stroked twice at 0.6 opacity, which becomes 0.84 opacity. In `deck/s3-dark.txt`, `deck/s9-dark.txt`, and `deck/s18-dark.txt`, because strands are sorted by $e$, inward `periapsis` branches and outward `apoapsis` branches are interleaved, and the viewer's eye is pulled inward and outward every 0.6 s. To fix this, drop the duplicate $e = 0$ `apoapsis` record in pulsating exports and order `periapsis` from highest $e$ down to $0$ before `apoapsis` upward in $e$.

3. On template slide 18 in dark and light modes across the libration points $\mathcal{L}_1$ and $\mathcal{L}_2$, I found a major timing distortion in `#multipliers`. In `template/s18-dark-f018.png` through `template/s18-dark-f021.png`, the readout for the amplitude $A$ is frozen at $A = 0.124$ across four consecutive captures while a viewer sees the unit-circle pair accelerate to 38 degrees per second near $\zeta = -1$. Past $\zeta = -1$ between `template/s18-dark-f019.png` and `template/s18-dark-f020.png`, the unstable multiplier $\lambda_{\mathrm{h}}$ is stepped from $-1.000$ to $-1.476$ in two 70 ms intervals, at 590 pixels per second on $\mathcal{L}_1$ and 870 pixels per second on $\mathcal{L}_2$. In `demo/multipliers.mjs`, 51 extra frames on $\mathcal{L}_1$ and 47 on $\mathcal{L}_2$ were inserted only for $|\zeta| \le 1$ under a constant frame rate, so the halo event is reached at 0.91 s. To fix this, give plot frames explicit timestamps in $A$, or resample both $|\zeta| \le 1$ and $|\zeta| > 1$ uniformly in combined $(A, \lambda_{\mathrm{h}})$ arc length.

4. On talk slides 2 and 18 in light mode and on template slide 12 in dark and light modes, I measured a major loss of contrast against the slide background. In `deck/s18-light-still.png`, `deck/s2-light-still.png`, and `captures/s18-light-zstill.png`, Family 11 is blended onto cream RGB (250, 248, 243) at RGB (219, 196, 133) to RGB (183, 199, 133), where the contrast ratio is 1.62:1 to 1.72:1. In `template/s12-dark-still.png`, `template/s12-light-still.png`, and `captures/template/s12-dark-zstill.png`, I measured 1.63:1 and 1.78:1 on the dark-mode outer loops and 1.62:1 and 1.84:1 on the light-mode inner loops, because lightness is shifted in `shadeOf` (`src/strands.ts`) without checking the theme. To fix this, darken light-mode `--strand-5` and `--strand-6` by about 0.14 in OKLab lightness, and invert or clamp the lightness ramp in `shadeOf` per mode so every strand is kept above 2.8:1 contrast.

5. On talk slide 2 and template slide 12 in dark and light modes, I found a major legibility clash where unshielded text is crossed. In `deck/s2-dark-still.png`, `deck/s2-light-still.png`, `template/s12-dark-still.png`, and `template/s12-light-still.png`, the slide heading, `.caption`, and `.foot` are placed directly over 0.6-opacity curves without a background mask, unlike the prose blocks on `.statement` and `.closing` slides. To fix this, add a soft `--bg` radial scrim or `paint-order: stroke fill` halo to `h2`, `.caption`, and `.foot` whenever `.figure` is paired with `.backdrop`.

6. On talk slides 3, 9, and 18 in dark and light modes, I found a minor framing and pacing flaw on the outermost `apoapsis` orbits. In `deck/s3-dark-f015.png` through `deck/s3-dark-f022.png`, `deck/s9-dark-f015.png` through `deck/s9-dark-f022.png`, and `deck/s18-dark-f016.png` through `deck/s18-dark-f022.png`, only 35 to 44 percent of the outermost strand is on-screen, and the trace is hidden off-screen for 4.0 to 4.5 s. To fix this, lower the upper `apoapsis` cutoff in $e$ in `backdrops/export_broucke_families.py` so at least 65 percent of each loop is visible, or roll clipped strands to start inside the viewport.

7. On talk slides 1 and 22 and template slide 1 in dark and light modes, I found minor collisions between orbits and display text. In `deck/s1-dark-still.png` and `template/s1-dark-still.png`, I see orange strand 181 cross the subtitle, and in `deck/s22-dark-still.png` I see the bright left oval touch "kind" and "theory" between horizontal pixels 1305 and 1395. To fix this, narrow `.closing .block` so text is kept left of pixel 1280, and nudge `data-cx` slightly left on slide 1 or add a subtle `--bg` text halo.

8. On talk slide 1 and template slide 1 in dark and light modes, I found a minor slowdown of the transfer head inside the right-hand halo loop. Across `deck/s1-dark-f033.png` through `deck/s1-dark-f047.png` (16.5 s to 23.5 s), the glowing head is confined near pixel (1760, 655) at roughly 70 pixels per second, after moving across the transfer arc at over 300 pixels per second. To fix this, compress the normalized clock along halo strands 182 and 183 in `brand/transfer.js` so the screen-space speed of the head is kept more even.

9. On talk slide 1 and template slide 1 in dark and light modes, I found a nit at two oblique-projection cusps of the transfer curve. In `deck/s1-dark-still.png` near pixels (1214, 893) and (1764, 624), I measured 2.3 to 7.6 pixel segments turning through 131 to 172 degrees in `brand/transfer.js` where the three-dimensional tangent is nearly aligned with the sightline. To fix this, subdivide projected cusps to sub-pixel chords when exporting `brand/transfer.js`.

Drop the duplicate zero-eccentricity `apoapsis` orbit and sweep `periapsis` inward before `apoapsis` outward in `backdrops/export_broucke_families.py`, while giving `#multipliers` in `demo/multipliers.mjs` explicit timestamps or uniform amplitude-and-multiplier arc-length steps across `zeta = -1`.
