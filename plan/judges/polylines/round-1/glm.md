## Findings, most severe first

1. On template slide 18, in both modes, the multipliers pair rushes through ζ = −1, the period-doubling bifurcation the talk is about. Severity: major. Evidence: my measurement of the JSON block `id="multipliers"` in the template's `index.html`, and the frames behind `s18-dark-f018-minusone` and `s18-dark-f019-minusone`. On the unit circle the pair steps 2.97° per frame near ±173°, the 3° cap of `demo/multipliers.mjs`, against 1.65° per frame between ±60° and ±120°, i.e. about 43 against 24 degrees per second at 14.4 frames per second, and the tour tracked 38 against 22 degrees per second; the L2 tab steps at the same cap near −1. The fact reported with the changes, "hence the pair is slower near −1", is the opposite of what the frames do, since frames inserted at even angle steps cap the step and thus play the dense raw motion near −1 at the cap while the sparse motion mid-circle plays slower. Fix: give the inserted frames a smaller angle cap within about 30° of ±1 (for instance 1°) or ease the frame clock, so the pair slows toward −1 as intended.

2. On deck slides 3, 9 and 18, in both modes, the trace ends at the earliest moment the speaker might leave, and the requested sequential reading is a rolling wave. Severity: major. Evidence: the transcripts `s3-dark.txt`, `s9-dark.txt` and `s18-dark.txt` put the last finish at 20.0, 20.0 and 21.0 s at the 0.5 s capture resolution, and `index.polylines.judge.html` gives the three backdrops `data-stagger="0.6"` and `data-duration="7"`. A member is traced over 7 s while the next starts 0.6 s later, so about twelve members are in flight at once from 7 to 13 s. Template slide 12 behaves alike and finishes at 18.0 s. Fix: set `data-duration` to 5 or 6, or `data-stagger` to 0.45, which ends each trace by about 15 to 16 s and thins the wave.

3. On deck slide 2, in both modes, the first four seconds still fill the left half of the plate first. Severity: minor. Evidence: my simulation from `backdrops/broucke-families.json` gives 1.78 times the drawn arc length left of the window center against right at 0.5 s, 1.54 at 2 s and 1.2 from 5.5 s, and the tour measured 1.81, 1.17 and 1.19 in pixels (`s2-dark-f004-tips`, `s2-dark-still-center`). The hard top-to-bottom cut is gone, but the arc length of a member per unit of true anomaly is largest near apoapsis, which lies on the left in the barycentric inertial frame, and all members move at the same rate in anomaly. Fix: choose the start fractions against that bias, stratified by arc length, or let the first second of the trace fade in.

4. On deck and template slides 1 and 22, in both modes, the trace does not match its still at the end. Severity: minor. Evidence: the tour reports about 450 px of ink altered at 30.0 s on slide 1 (`s1-dark-f059-stacking` against `s1-dark-still-stacking`) and about 500 px of the ovals at 12.0 s on slide 22, with the glow more even after. The cause is in `src/orbits.ts`: `frame()` bakes each finished strand onto the under canvas in finish order, while the final `redraw()` restores file order. Both slides are the reference works, so I read this as a trait of the renderer rather than a defect of this round. Fix: bake finished strands in file order, or hold the still order during the trace.

5. On deck slide 18, in both modes, only one primary is in view. Severity: minor.
