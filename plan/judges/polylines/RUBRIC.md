# Rubric: the polyline backdrops

Score the work as a whole against the reference of `BRIEF.md`, from 1 to 10, after you have scored each criterion below from 1 to 10. Name the slide, the mode, and the frame or crop on which each judgment rests.

## The criteria

1. Smoothness of each curve. Is every curve smooth at the size it is shown, without a visible polygon, a jagged corner, or a chord across a loop? Look at the crops at twice the resolution, at the outer members of the section slides, and at the arc near ζ = −1 on slide 18 of the template.
2. The trace. While a strand is drawn, does it look like the final still, without beads, dots, notches, or flicker? Does the line end at its head, where a head is drawn, without a lag or a jump? Does the glow of a glowing strand stay even?
3. Starts and ends. Do the strands start and close without a seam, i.e. without a line or a point where many strands start or end at once, and is each closed orbit closed in the still? On slide 2 of the talk deck, does the screen still look cut in half?
4. Order and pace. On the section slides 3, 9, and 18 of the talk deck, and on slide 12 of the template, are the members drawn one after another at a pace the eye can follow, and does the whole drawing end well before the speaker leaves the slide (about 20 to 30 s)? Does any part stall or rush, including the pair of multipliers near ζ = −1 on slide 18 of the template?
5. Color and weight. Are the colors of each family and the ramp along e right in both modes, are the weights of the lines even, and does a strand keep its color from the trace to the still?
6. Composition. Is each drawing framed so that no strand is cut at an edge of its box inside the slide, and does it sit well beside the words of its slide?
7. Match to the reference. Would a viewer of `tubes`, of the title transfer, and of 3Blue1Brown take these backdrops as made by the same hand?

## The report

Write `report.md` in your folder in this shape.

- A first line `Overall: N/10`, with your score against the reference.
- A table of the seven criteria with a score each and one sentence of reason.
- Numbered findings, most severe first. Each finding has a severity (blocker, major, minor, or nit), the slide and mode, the evidence (a frame or crop file of the evidence tour or of your own captures, or a measurement), what is wrong, and what change would fix it.
- The three changes that would raise your score most, in order.

End your reply with the same `Overall: N/10` line and the same three changes.
