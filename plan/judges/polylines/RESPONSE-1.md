# Response to round 1

Four judges scored round 1 at 7/10 each: Codex sol, Gemini Argon, GLM, and Claude Opus on the Enterprise seat. Claude Opus on Antigravity was not available, since the quota of that seat was used up. Below, each finding is answered with what was changed for round 2 or why it was rejected. The findings that several judges made are answered once, with the judges named.

## Accepted and changed

1. The still differs from the last frame of the trace (all four judges). On slides 1 and 22 the strands overlapped in another order during the trace than in the still, and on slides 3, 18, and 22 the glow and the brightness of finished strands changed when the still was painted. The renderer was changed so that the last frame of a trace is identical to the still, with the strands overlapping in file order throughout.
   Outcome: the offscreen canvas of finished strands was removed, and every started strand is drawn in file order at every frame. The implementer measured that the pixels changed between the last frame and the still, away from the heads, fell from between 1,880 and 16,234 to none on template slides 1, 12, and 22 and on deck slides 3, 9, and 18. A new test fails on the old renderer.
2. The pair of multipliers on template slide 18 was moved fastest just before ζ = −1, split in about 0.3 s, and jumped back to +1 at the restart (all four judges). A tab of a plot can now carry a time per frame, and the plot was retimed so that the pair slows down toward −1, pauses there, splits over about 1.5 s, and is held split before the loop restarts, with the traces faded out and in at the restart.
   Outcome: after the axial crossing (5.0 s on L1, 5.65 s on L2) the pair is moved at 30°/s and slowed evenly to 4°/s at −1. It rests there 0.5 s, splits along the real axis over 1.5 s, and is held 3 s before a fade of 0.5 s out and in. Live samples near −1 give 12.6 to 4.4°/s, against 164 to 205°/s in round 1, and one loop now lasts about 18 s.
3. Strands of slide 2 stall and then sweep, and the outer members of the section slides are rushed (Opus finding 1 and 4), and slide 2 fills its left half first (GLM finding 3). Each strand is now traced at an even speed along its own length on screen, with a short ease at both ends, and every strand of a drawing takes the same time.
   Outcome: the clock of every record is its normalized length along the polyline, eased over the first and the last tenth. The implementer measured the tips at 16 to 418 px/s on slide 2, against 0 to 10,039 px/s in round 1, and at 518 to 1,625 px/s on slides 3, 9, and 18.
4. Too many members in flight on the section slides, and an end too close to the time the slide is shown (Codex finding 2, GLM finding 2, Opus finding 5). The stagger and the duration of slides 3, 9, and 18 were set so that each drawing ends by about 15 s with at most about five members in flight.
   Outcome: under Claudio's decision of 9 s, slides 3, 9, and 18 draw every other member of their family (10, 10, and 11 members), 0.6 s apart and in 3 s each, with at most five in flight. Their transcripts end at 8.4, 8.4, and 9.0 s. Drawing every member in 9 s would have moved the tips at up to 3,100 to 4,700 px/s.
5. The two branches interleaved on the section slides, and the member at e = 0 drawn twice (Gemini finding 2). The members of each family are now ordered so that a staggered trace sweeps through the family in one motion, and the member at e = 0 is drawn once.
   Outcome: each family runs down the periapsis branch from its largest e to 0, then up the apoapsis branch, and its pulsating record at e = 0 is written once.
6. The starts of the members on the section slides, and the outermost members drawn off screen (Opus finding 5, Gemini finding 6, Opus finding 4). A common start on screen was tried against the scattered starts on the pulsating records.
   Outcome: on the section slides each member is begun where it crosses a ray at 150° from the secondary, so that the members in flight form one fan at the upper left. In a live comparison, scattered starts left the tips at unrelated places. On slide 2, which is traced all at once, every strand is begun at a golden-ratio fraction of its period in both frames.
7. Low contrast of family 11 in light mode and of the ends of the shade ramp of template slide 12, and faint outer members of family 8 (Gemini finding 4, Opus finding 7). Every color of the strand palette and every end of a ramp or a shade was measured against the background at the opacity of a strand, and raised to at least about 2.5:1 where it was lower.
   Outcome: four light colors of the palette were darkened at their hue to 2.60 to 2.70:1, and the shades were moved off the background to at least 2.50:1. On the deck and on template slide 12 the palette is replaced by the colormaps of Claudio's figures, drawn at an opacity of 0.87, at which the worst point of each ramp has 2.52:1 or more in both modes.
8. The words "kind" and "theory" over the left oval of the deck's closing slide (Opus finding 3, Gemini finding 7). The paragraph of this slide was narrowed, and the tubes were kept where they are.
   Outcome: the paragraph is narrowed to 1,125 px by a style and keeps its five lines, and its nearest line end is about 71 px left of the left oval.
9. The turn of family 7 cut by the top and bottom edges of template slide 12, and its trace a fill rather than a sequence (Opus finding 8, Codex findings 1 and 2). Slide 12 was given the pace of the section slides and reframed.
   Outcome: slide 12 draws its 16 members 0.6 s apart and in 3 s each, with at most five in flight, and ends at 12.0 s. Its window was moved to the center (−0.99, 0) with a half height of 1.8, and the drawing lies within x 1093 to 1810 px and y 100 to 977 px.

## Rejected

1. Reframe every drawing so that no strand runs off the slide, and keep the drawings clear of the words on slide 2 and template slide 12 (Codex finding 1, Gemini finding 5). The theme lets a drawing run off the slide at an edge of the slide, as `tubes` does, and requires only that it not be cut at an edge of its box inside the slide, which the judges found held. Slide 2 and template slide 12 are figure plates, on which the backdrop fills the slide behind the heading and the caption by design. A scrim behind the text of every figure plate is a change of the theme beyond this brief, and it is listed for Claudio.
2. The sharp corners of `tubes` on slide 22 (Codex finding 3). Opus and GLM measured them as true cusps of the arcs of the tubes, and `tubes` is the reference itself.
3. The transfer of slide 1 drawn across words of the title, its slow head inside the halo loop, and its projected cusps (Opus finding 9, Gemini findings 7 to 9). The title transfer is a reference that Claudio chose, its data were computed elsewhere, and the pause of the head is part of it.
4. Only one primary in view on deck slide 18 (GLM finding 5, Opus finding 10). The primary of family 11 lies outside the box of the drawing at the fitted window, which is correct, and drawing both would shrink the members.
5. A moiré in the band where the members of family 8 nearly coincide (GLM finding 6, Opus finding 7). The band is the bunching of the family itself. Its saturation is answered by item 7 above, and an opacity that varies with e was not added, since it would be a new feature of the renderer.
