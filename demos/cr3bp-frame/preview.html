The circular restricted three-body problem

The primary and the secondary circle their barycenter, and a third body, too light to move them, flies among them.

We take their separation as the unit of length, and the time unit such that they turn once in \(2\pi\).

In the frame turning with them, both are at rest,

\[m_1 \text{ at } (-\mu, 0), \qquad m_2 \text{ at } (1 - \mu, 0),\]

where the mass ratio \(\mu = m_2/(m_1 + m_2)\) is the only parameter.

The third body feels both, at the distances \(\rho_1\) and \(\rho_2\).
